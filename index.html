About Me

I'm a cybersecurity student with a hacker's mindset — I believe the best defenders are those who think like attackers.

Currently pursuing a BCA in Cybersecurity at Somaiya Vidyavihar University, I spend my free time breaking into intentionally vulnerable machines on TryHackMe, analyzing network traffic with Wireshark, and studying real-world CVEs to understand how attackers think.

My approach to cybersecurity is methodical: understand the attack surface, enumerate carefully, exploit precisely, and document everything. I'm particularly drawn to web application security, network forensics, and the science of malware behavior.

Attack Mindset Understanding threats from the adversary's perspective
